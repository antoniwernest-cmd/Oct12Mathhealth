Excite Work Bench A and Inspire Work Bench A

Work Bench A has 45 individual number questions per grade. Work Bench B has 30 visual multiplication questions per grade, separate from the five daily activities. Work Bench D has 30 fair-sharing questions per grade. Children choose the number of pencils and children, then share in up to three turns. The Can’t share equally pad holds leftover whole pencils. Checks review each child’s total and the pad, and reject leftovers that could be shared in another round. Each grade has 20 exact-share and 10 leftover problems. Grade 2 B2.6 uses up to 12 objects; Grade 3 B2.6 uses division within 100 by divisors up to 10. Work Bench E has 30 addition questions per grade. Children use quiet grey sliders to split either or both addends, then split any part again. Every parts row is ordered biggest to smallest, with five answer buttons. Grade 2 B2.4 uses sums to 100; Grade 3 B2.5 uses sums to 1000. Work Bench F has 30 counting-on questions per grade. Children start with a numeral, then tap each emoji once to count one more and choose from five totals. Grade 2 B2.4 uses sums to 100; Grade 3 B2.5 uses sums to 1000. The Excel workbook has twelve Work Bench assessment tabs, six answer guides and a 520-question expectation map. Work Bench A uses quantities to 200 for Excite and to 1000 for Inspire.

Weekly count-on subtraction · Days 1–5

Ten interactive activities with two fresh problems each. Children count on from the smaller number to the larger number, then choose the difference. The blue start counts as zero moves. Each selected next square counts as one move. The strips keep consecutive numbers together across tens and hundreds. No completed moves are shown in advance.

Children can undo a move, clear one row or start the task again. They may count beyond the target; no automatic success cue gives the answer. Checks require the correct counted endpoint and difference for both rows. Counting and answers save with the day’s work and appear in response exports.

Excite · Grade 2 · B2.4: a number-strip diagram and equations for subtraction situations within 100. Inspire · Grade 3 · B2.5: use a number strip to solve subtraction problems within 1000. These tasks practise small differences between nearby numbers; they do not cover the full expectation by themselves.

Work Bench E · PNG print pages

Each of the 30 Excite and 30 Inspire decomposition questions has a Print / PNG button. It includes the current splits, coloured blocks and chosen total. Save the image, or print a landscape Letter sheet. If a learner creates more than eight parts on one side, extra sheets preserve every part.

Weekly bar graphs · Days 1–5

Ten native activity pages with four questions each: read a bar, identify the fewest, find a difference and make a choice based on the data. The shop, book, flower, fruit and game contexts use new made-up data. No answer is selected in advance. Start again clears the four choices. Normal daily checking, progress, Finished and response exports apply.

Excite · Grade 2 · D1.5: analyse data in bar graphs by answering questions, drawing conclusions and making decisions. Each square represents one item or vote. Inspire · Grade 3 · D1.5: analyse graphs with different scales. Each square represents 2 or 5 items or votes. The scale choice follows the one-to-one / many-to-one progression in D1.3; graph construction is not assessed here. These pages practise part of D1.5, not the full expectation.

Placed cards leave the card bank and appear once as movable cards on their pads. Select or drag a placed card to move it to another group; use Return card to put it back in the bank.
