MATH MALL 1

Open index.html or START-MATH-MALL.html.
Click your school-supply student ID to enter. All 20 school supplies are available.
Use the same supply each time to return to that student's saved answers and Finished ticks.
Choose Excite (Grade 2) or Inspire (Grade 3), then a day or Work Bench A-G.
One Math question appears at a time. Use Previous question and Next question.

Answers save in this browser separately for each student ID, grade and activity.
Answer button order is randomized once and stays steady while the student works.
Allow Check answer is one shared setting for the entire Math Mall project.
Save responses includes the school-supply student ID in the downloaded CSV.
Reset the whole project clears all students, all daily pages and all Work Benches after confirmation.
Save any responses you want to keep before using reset.

Keep the Math Mall 1 folder together, including assets/supplies.
Teacher-Resources.html contains the teacher resources. Parent PDF links are on the entry page.

Day 5 now includes two C1.4 pattern-creation questions per level: growing and shrinking patterns. Excite Day 5 has 17 questions; Inspire Day 5 has 16.

WORK BENCHES H AND I
Work Bench H: 30 number-line addition/subtraction activities per grade. Excite B2.4 within 100; Inspire B2.5 within 1000. Work Bench I: 30 number-line rounding activities per grade. Inspire B1.3 uses nearest tens and hundreds. Excite nearest-ten rounding is an explicitly labelled Grade 2 extension, practising B1.1/B1.2 representation and comparison through 200. Every question includes its Ontario expectations popup. Both new benches use school-supply student IDs and whole-project reset. Open the HTML files or use the entry page. Browser Print prints the new activities; earlier PDFs and Excel exports keep their existing content.
