Health Centre 1

Open index.html. Choose Excite or Inspire, then a day.
The entry page has compact day buttons. Each day link starts at question 1;
saved answers and Finished ticks are kept. Explicit question links still work.
Each of the five days has 10 Health questions.
No login or typed student answers. Use Next question and Next step.
Tap Check my answers and tick Finished. Answers save separately by grade and day.
Save my Health day downloads just that day’s 10 responses.
New person? Start fresh clears only this project after confirmation.
Teacher-Resources.html links this project’s PDF and Excel files.
The student PDF has Excite on pages 1–56 and Inspire on pages 57–112.
Day covers: Excite 2, 13, 24, 35, 46; Inspire 58, 69, 80, 91, 102.
Each HTML includes its pictures, fonts and scripts and works offline.
Keep this project folder together. No ZIP is needed.

Every Health question has an Expectation button with a trillium above the label.
Press it for the related official Ontario Grade 2 or Grade 3 expectation wording.
Use See all Grade 2/3 Health expectations for the complete grade reference list.
The entry page also has an Expectation button for each grade.
Each grade includes all 13 specific Healthy Living expectations, 3 overall
Healthy Living expectations, and the overall plus 6 specific social-emotional
learning expectations. Social-emotional examples are omitted in the popup;
the linked source page contains the full examples.
Reference-only expectations are identified when no current activity is linked.
Related activities may address only part of an expectation.
Ontario-Health-Physical-Education-2019.pdf is the supplied official source.
PDF links show both printed curriculum page and PDF page numbers.
