Health Centre 1 · Teacher resources

Five independent days for Excite Grade 2 and Inspire Grade 3. Each day has 10 Health questions, numbered 1–10, and its own assessment.

  • Health Excel assessment
  • Health assessment PDF, answer guide and expectations
  • Health student PDF · both grades

Completion and automatic checks do not assign teacher achievement levels. Record support and evidence for each day.

Food choices, oral health, personal safety, feelings, respect and online safety appear in the Health activities. Inspire also includes concussion awareness.
